# Phase 03 · Design research

**Review date: 18 November 2026.** This is the pre-implementation evidence record. The [implemented UI](UI-DESIGN.md) records the later delivery. The subject is the launcher window a player sees before starting Vanilla Java Edition. The [interaction specification](UI-UX-SPECIFICATION.md) describes the proposed experience.

## Method and limits

Read the local manifest, startup wiring, theme, disclosure state, keyboard mapping, Home view and Phase 02 records first. Compared the publishers' own descriptions with their public visuals. Inspected the three primary promotional screenshots in memory, repository trees, source excerpts and component-specific licenses. No launcher binary was run. Screenshots show particular releases and cannot establish responsiveness, keyboard behavior or usability in practice. The observations below describe the evidence inspected. The transfer and exclusion columns are design judgments.

Used the installed UI/UX Pro Max offline design-system query for “minecraft game launcher atmospheric dark” and a focused UX query for “carousel keyboard motion”. Retained explicit focus, measured foreground/background pairs, bounded motion, consistent spacing and a single dominant action. The generated green/gold marketing palette, repeated calls to action and landing-page sections conflict with the selected blue/cyan identity and desktop launch workflow. The skill lists no Iced stack. Checked actual crate features and source separately. [Skill](../.agents/skills/ui-ux-pro-max/SKILL.md) · [Iced feasibility](ICED-DESIGN-FEASIBILITY.md)

## Asset provenance and release decision

| Candidate source | Owner and permission evidence | Attribution and scope | Packaging recommendation |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Official Trails & Tales update](https://www.minecraft.net/en-us/article/the-trails---tales-update) or other official release pages | Minecraft/Mojang/Microsoft or credited licensors. Official publication identifies provenance. No asset-specific grant for this launcher's source distribution, installers or modified backgrounds was found. | Credit or a non-affiliation notice alone cannot grant permission. Identify actual owner for each image and obtain terms covering cropping, blur, darkening, thumbnails and redistribution by downstream distributors. | Keep official art outside all bundles until a grant covers the intended use. |
| Original Averion terrain illustration or commission | Artist initially owns the work subject to agreement. Proposed source only. Obtain an assignment or explicit license covering source repository, binaries, mirrors, modification and redistribution. | Preserve creator and required credits. Use independently created stepped geology, lighting and materials. Exclude game textures, logos, characters, extracted assets and traced key art. | Cleared original art can supply release-family scenery and the universal fallback. A solid slate field works before an asset clears. |
| [Lucide icon set and license](https://lucide.dev/license) | Lucide contributors, with Feather-derived icons credited to their respective authors. ISC and MIT notices apply to the respective works. | Retain copyright, permission and disclaimer notices for the chosen subset in distributions. Record icon names, upstream revision and applicable notices. | Candidate for common navigation symbols. Original text wordmark and avatar monogram cover branding and missing profile art. No icon files acquired. |

The Minecraft EULA permits independent tools under conditions and reserves redistribution of Mojang-made material. The usage guidelines give context-specific permissions and require care with naming, affiliation and notices. Provisions for publications, screenshots or videos do not establish a general software-bundling grant. Free distribution and preprocessing do not resolve rights. Keep Averion the dominant brand, describe compatibility secondarily and use the applicable prominent non-affiliation notice before public distribution. Keep a per-asset rights record distinct from the project's unresolved software license. News design uses source labels and links. Reproducing article copy or thumbnails also needs an appropriate basis. [Minecraft EULA](https://www.minecraft.net/en-us/eula) · [Minecraft Usage Guidelines](https://www.minecraft.net/en-us/usage-guidelines)

### Delivered artwork provenance

The implementation uses independently authored terrain geometry, palettes, a wordmark symbol, a generic portrait and seven navigation symbols. Terrain planes are deterministic renders of the bundled authoring script. The navigation strip is rasterized from the repository's own SVG. No Minecraft promotional image, launcher screenshot, skin, game texture, third-party font or icon library contributed pixels. The scene names describe original terrain studies. They do not depict official release artwork. A future project-wide distribution license remains the owner's decision.

The authoring step uses Python and ImageMagick 7. Run `python3 assets/terrain/generate.py` from the repository root to reproduce the terrain, or `magick -background none assets/icons/navigation.svg -depth 8 rgba:assets/icons/navigation.rgba` to rebuild the icon strip. Only the checked-in RGBA planes are needed to compile and run. [Terrain source](../assets/terrain/generate.py) · [Icon source](../assets/icons/navigation.svg)
